**S09 - Three conflicts, one ordered rule file, then one more rule.** In an invented optics team three pairs of proposals claim the same resource in the same week. Under `rules/arbitration.json` the earlier due date wins the first, and the other two (same due date; two safety claims) are covered by no rule and go to a human. A second rule file (`input/rules_v2/`) adds exactly one rule for the tie. The check passes only if exactly one outcome changes, the conflict no rule covers is still escalated, the dissent against the arbitration is recorded, and the register is append-only: the new record supersedes the old one, which is left byte for byte as it was. Run: `python scenarios/S09_arbitration_rules/check.py` - last line `S09_arbitration_rules PASS` or `S09_arbitration_rules FAIL`. Inputs: `input/` (git history as fast-import streams, written by `scenarios/make_inputs.py`). Expected: `expected/declared.json` (written by hand in `scenarios/stories.py`) and `expected/gold*.json` (reference reducer). Everything here is synthetic.
